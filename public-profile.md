# Public Profile <a target="_blank" href="https://tradermake.money/app2/settings/?tab=public-profile" class="btn btn-header">Go to public profile</a>

A public profile is your personal trading business card. It allows you to showcase your performance, build a reputation, and compete with other traders while maintaining full control over what data is revealed.

Your public profile consists of:
- **Bio & Social Links**: A profile with your contact information and trading philosophy.
- **Custom Widgets**: A fully customizable dashboard showing your performance metrics.
- **Trade History**: An optional, verifiable list of your trades.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Can I hide specific trades from my profile?</div>
<div class="faq-answer">No. To maintain transparency and trust within the community, you cannot hide or "cherry-pick" individual trades. You can only use the <strong>"Hide trades before date"</strong> setting to start your public history from a specific point in time.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Can I remove the Cumulative Profit widget?</div>
<div class="faq-answer">No. The Cumulative Profit widget is mandatory for all public profiles. It ensures that viewers can see the overall trend and consistency of a trader, preventing the spread of misleading or partial performance data.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Can I apply filters to my public widgets?</div>
<div class="faq-answer">No. Unlike the private Summary section, public widgets do not support custom filters. This ensures that the data shown is a true representation of the selected API keys' performance.</div>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Does archiving trades in my journal affect my public profile?</div>
<div class="faq-answer">No. Archiving trades only hides them from your private view. The public profile pulls data directly from the exchange via your API keys to ensure all stats are verifiable.</div>
</div>

</div>

## Configuration Steps

### Step 1: Visibility & Data
In the **Settings** tab of the advanced configuration, you define the "rules" for your profile:

<!-- panels:start -->
<!-- div:left-panel -->

- **API Keys**: Choose which accounts to include in your public stats.
- **Custom Link**: Create a unique URL for your profile.
- **Hide History**: Set a "start public history from" date. All trades before this date will be hidden, but a note will appear on your profile indicating that earlier history is not displayed.
- **Profit Visibility**: Choose whether to show absolute profit values ($) or keep them private (showing only %).
- **Trade List**: Decide if you want to show all trades, only closed ones, or hide the list entirely.

### Step 2: Profile Design
In the **Profile** tab, you can customize your "brand":

<!-- panels:start -->
<!-- div:left-panel -->

- **Visuals**: Upload a custom avatar and a wide background image (recommended 1920x500px).
- **Bio**: Write a description of your trading style.
- **Social Media**: Link your Twitter, Telegram, Discord, and other platforms so followers can reach you.
- **Live Preview**: See exactly how your profile looks as you make changes.

### Step 3: Public Widgets
Just like the [Summary](summary.md) section, you can build a custom dashboard for your public viewers:

<!-- panels:start -->
<!-- div:left-panel -->

- **Add Widgets**: Choose from a wide variety of charts and metrics.
- **Privacy Mode**: For sensitive widgets, you can enable [Private Mode](summary.md#private-mode) to show relative trends instead of exact numbers.
- **Mandatory Widget**: The **Accumulative Profit** widget is often used as a baseline to verify long-term consistency.

### Privacy & Transparency
- **Verifiable History**: You cannot hide individual trades within your chosen period. This ensures that every public profile is honest and trustworthy.
- **Starting Over**: If you want a "clean slate," use the **Hide trades before date** setting to restart your public equity curve from today.

> **Disclaimer**: Posting offensive content or misleading images can result in a permanent ban of your public profile. Please review our [terms of use](https://tradermake.money/terms-and-conditions).
