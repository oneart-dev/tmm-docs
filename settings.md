## Referral Program

<!-- panels:start -->
<!-- div:left-panel -->

Invite others and earn subscription rewards.
- **Friend's Benefit**: Your invited friend receives a **20% discount** on their first payment.
- **Your Reward**: You receive **1 month of subscription** as a bonus for the first payment of each invited friend.
- **Once Per Account**: The bonus is granted only once per referred user, regardless of how many times they renew.
- **Fixed Bonus**: You earn 1 month regardless of the friend's payment duration (even for annual plans).
- **Auto-Extension**: Bonuses automatically extend your current subscription status.
- **Status if inactive**: If you don't have a sub, you'll receive **Beginner Plus** (if friend buys basic) or **Trader** (if friend buys Trader/PRO).
- **Cash Withdrawal**: You can request a withdrawal of unused bonuses to your personal wallet by emailing [info@tradermake.money](mailto:info@tradermake.money).
